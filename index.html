How can AI automation help a local business?

It can cut repetitive admin, organise information and help teams respond faster, with people still checking the results.

What is SideQuest Tourism?

SideQuest Tourism is a tourism app built by ByteWave for discovering Brunei. Travellers find local experiences, book them, collect stamps in a digital passport and pay from one travel wallet, and local tourism businesses get a place to be found. It launches in Brunei first, with more destinations planned later.

What is QRskan?

QRskan is an event ticketing platform built by ByteWave in Brunei. Organisers create events, send digital invitations and run lucky draws, and guests open their tickets with a code sent to their email and get in by QR check-in. It works in English and Malay.

Where is ByteWave Digital based?

ByteWave Digital Enterprise is based in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei Darussalam. We work with businesses, organisations and founders across Brunei.

Do you build MVPs and early products?

Yes. We help founders and organisations go from an early idea to a working first version they can put in front of users.
