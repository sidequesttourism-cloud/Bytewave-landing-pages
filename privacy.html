Privacy Policy

ByteWave Digital Enterprise · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information ByteWave Digital Enterprise ("ByteWave", "we") collects through bytewave-digitalbrunei.com, why we collect it, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We aim to handle personal data in line with Brunei's Personal Data Protection Order 2025.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you send the project inquiry form on the Start a project page, we receive:

  • your name, contact number and email address
  • your organisation, if you add it
  • the services you select
  • the description of your idea
  • the estimated budget you choose, and your confirmation that pricing is agreed after our discussion

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp instead, we receive whatever you include in that message. We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through this website.

Why we use it

We use your details only to read your enquiry, reply to you (including by calling or messaging the number you give us) and discuss the work you asked about. A budget you choose is only a guide to help that discussion; it is not an offer or a quote. By sending the form you consent to this. We do not sell your information, share it for marketing, or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) receives the form and forwards it to our email inbox. Your message passes through its servers, which may be located outside Brunei. Web3Forms' own privacy policy covers its handling.
  • Our email provider stores the enquiry once it reaches our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, as any web host does, and may keep them briefly in its security logs.
  • WhatsApp: if you open a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to that conversation.

The fonts, images and code on this site are served from our own website, so loading a page does not send your details to font or analytics companies.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies, does not use advertising trackers and does not run an analytics product. We do not build a profile of your browsing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to deal with your request and keep a reasonable record of our correspondence. We delete enquiries that do not lead to work when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to stop contacting you. Email Aziq@bytewave-digitalbrunei.com and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and we keep the information we hold to what we need to reply to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will take reasonable steps to contain it and will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
